Yellow Rust (Puccinia striiformis)

About the Disease

Yellow rust is a fungal disease that affects wheat. It appears as yellow-orange pustules in linear rows on the leaves.

Severity Status

High Risk

Recommended Solutions & Treatment

  • Apply Propiconazole 25% EC @ 500 ml/ha.
  • Use rust-resistant wheat varieties for the next season.
  • Early sowing to avoid peak infection periods.
